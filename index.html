About

I am a registered mechanical engineer with a B.Sc in Mechanical Engineering from Lagos State University, registered with the Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria (COREN). I lead the workshop at The Wagon Car Garage in Osogbo, where I combine engineering knowledge with hands-on diagnosis and repair.

I have led technician teams, rebuilt engines, and worked as a course instructor and school teacher, so I am used to turning complex mechanical problems into simple terms for people who have never looked under a bonnet.

I speak English and Yoruba fluently.

What I do

Work I handle every week on cars, SUVs and light trucks.

  • Fault diagnosis

    Inspections, test drives and diagnostic software to find the real cause of performance and safety problems before any part is replaced.

  • Electrical systems

    Diagnosis and repair of vehicle electrical faults using computer programs and the manufacturer's electrical diagrams.

  • Engine rebuilds and replacement

    Engine rebuilding and engine replacement, including removing and replacing faulty components such as transmissions and brakes.

    Every repair, maintenance job and part used is documented accurately for your records.
